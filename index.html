ABOUT ME

Creative at heart.
Communicator by craft.

I’m Jillian, a communications professional who brings together storytelling, strategy and creativity to create clear, engaging content that connects with people.

Born and raised in rural Alberta, I grew up surrounded by all things “yeehaw.” My creative journey started in Grade 4, when a teacher complimented one of my watercolour paintings. That small moment encouraged me to keep creating and sparked a lasting love of art, design and storytelling.

After discovering photojournalism in high school, I pursued a B.A. in Communications and Media Studies at the University of Calgary. Today, I bring that creative foundation to my work in communications, combining strategic thinking, content creation and community engagement to tell stories that are accessible, meaningful and audience-focused.

When I’m not creating content, you can usually spot me in one of my many fun sweaters, from cats doing yoga to colourful mountain landscapes.
